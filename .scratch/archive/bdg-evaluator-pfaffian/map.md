- U13 (periodic/Bloch BdG construction) stays as the explicit future scoped PR for the slim → full Bloch-Pfaffian swap. The BLOCKING-EMPIRICAL polarization gap (separate observable) stays as its own `@todo U13`.

The PR is "what we already have wired together" — the seam is shipped, the slim witness is shipped, the Kitaev wrapper is shipped; the work is wiring them through the seam and retiring the heuristic.
